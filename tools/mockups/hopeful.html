Our services

Supervised access, transport and outreach, delivered by people trained to hold the room when a family is at its most fragile.

01 Supervised Family Access Service Facilitates supervised visitation between individuals accessing our service and their families, within a controlled and safe environment.
02 Supervised Access Transport for Children Provides transport for individuals who have no way to attend the arranged family access with the rest of their family.
03 Community Outreach Support Person-centred community support, encouraging people to develop social skills as they integrate within their communities.
04 Emergency Outreach Support Urgent community support for individuals facing sudden, unplanned changes in their circumstances, whatever the notice given.
05 Emergency Support for Funders Urgent assistance for children facing sudden, unplanned changes, at the same standard however short the notice period.

Professional support for care services.

Hopeful Hearts provides vetted HCA and Social Care Worker relief personnel for healthcare and residential settings, together with nationwide resident appointment transport.

Healthcare assistants Dependable HCA relief services

Support for elderly care, nursing environments and specialised physical disability units.

  • Minimum QQI Level 5 qualification in Healthcare
  • Practical certification in Patient Moving & Handling
  • Training in Safeguarding Vulnerable Adults and Infection Control
  • Local professionals prepared to work within existing teams
Social care workers Specialised social care relief

Therapeutic relief staff for children’s residential services, foster care support and disability units.

  • Degree-qualified Social Care professionals
  • Experience supporting complex emotional needs and behaviour support plans
  • MAPA, CPI or Crisis Prevention framework certification
  • Vetting under Tusla’s Children First safeguarding guidelines
