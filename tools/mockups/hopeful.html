Our services

Supervised access, transport and outreach, delivered by people trained to hold the room when a family is at its most fragile.

01 Supervised Family Access Service Facilitates supervised visitation between individuals accessing our service and their families, within a controlled and safe environment.
02 Supervised Access Transport for Children Provides transport for individuals who have no way to attend the arranged family access with the rest of their family.
03 Community Outreach Support Person-centred community support, encouraging people to develop social skills as they integrate within their communities.
04 Emergency Outreach Support Urgent community support for individuals facing sudden, unplanned changes in their circumstances, whatever the notice given.
05 Emergency Support for Funders Urgent assistance for children facing sudden, unplanned changes, at the same standard however short the notice period.
